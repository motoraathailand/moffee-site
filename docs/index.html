Moffee

Counter order app for coffee shops

Take orders. Print tickets. Close the day.

Moffee (มอฟฟี่) is a simple tablet app for small cafés. Tap the menu photos, print a store copy and a customer copy, and finish the day with one closing report. It works without internet.

Made for a busy counter

  • One tap to order

    Big menu photos. One tap puts a drink in the cart with its standard options already chosen. Tap “Edit” to change sweetness or size.

  • Prints your tickets

    Store and customer tickets on ESC/POS receipt printers over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi/LAN. No printer yet? You get a preview you can save as an image.

  • Daily closing report

    One tap makes an A4 report as PDF or image: sales by payment tag, category, item and hour, expenses and a deposit check.

  • Safe for new staff

    Staff see only Order and History. Discounts, voids, expenses, closing and settings need the manager PIN.

  • Works offline

    Orders, menu and reports live on the tablet, so a weak connection never stops the counter.

  • Your menu, your look

    Menu with photos and options, your colours and logo, and five languages: Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi.

Tickets and the closing report

A customer ticket and the A4 daily closing report, both from sample orders.

Your sales stay yours

  • No ads at all.
  • No payment processing — card payments go through your own card terminal.
  • Data stays on your device. We run no server that collects sales data.
  • Staff never sign in.
