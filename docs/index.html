Take orders. Print tickets. Close the day.

Moffee (มอฟฟี่) is a simple, free counter order app for cafés, restaurants and shops. Tap the menu photos on a tablet or phone, print a store copy and a customer copy, and finish the day with one closing report. It works without internet.

Made for a busy counter

  • One tap to order

    Big menu photos on a tablet or phone. One tap puts an item in the cart with its standard options already chosen. Tap “Edit” to change the options.

  • Prints your tickets

    Store and customer tickets with your own design, on ESC/POS receipt printers over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi/LAN. No printer yet? You get a preview you can save as an image.

  • Daily closing report

    One tap makes an A4 closing report as PDF or image: sales by payment tag, category, item and hour, expenses and a deposit check.

  • Easy for new staff

    Big buttons. Staff see only Order and History. Discounts, voids, expenses, closing and settings need the manager PIN.

  • Works offline

    Orders, menu and reports live on your device, so a weak connection never stops the counter.

  • Your menu, your look

    Menu with photos and options, light or dark mode, accent colours and your logo, and five languages: Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi.

Free. After the official release, only small banner ads.

Moffee costs nothing to use. After the official Google Play release it is supported by small banner ads only: no full-screen or pop-up ads, nothing that covers the screen.

Good to know

  • Payments stay separate. Moffee has no payment integration: customers pay on your own separate payment terminal, and the app only notes the payment tag (such as PromptPay, Card or Cash).
  • The owner signs in with Google. The owner’s Google account (name and email) is used for sign-in and for backups to the owner’s own Google Drive (drive.file: Moffee sees only the files it creates).
  • Your sales stay yours. Orders and reports are stored on your device and your own Drive. We run no server that collects them.
  • After the official release, the small banner ads (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers. The test version has no ads. The privacy policy explains it in plain words.
